# Assignment 1: Difference Between KDD and Data Mining

## Introduction

- **KDD (Knowledge Discovery in Databases)** is the complete process of finding useful knowledge in large datasets.
- **Data Mining** is one step inside KDD. It applies algorithms to pull hidden patterns out of the data.


## Comparison Table

| #  | Basis                 | KDD (Knowledge Discovery in Databases)                                                       | Data Mining                                                           |
| :-: | --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1  | **Definition**        | The process of discovering useful knowledge from large datasets.                             | The process of extracting hidden patterns from large datasets.        |
| 2  | **Scope**             | Broader scope.                                                                               | Narrower scope.                                                       |
| 3  | **Purpose**           | To discover meaningful knowledge and insights.                                               | To identify patterns, trends, and relationships in data.              |
| 4  | **Process**           | Multiple steps: data selection, cleaning, transformation, mining, and evaluation.            | Mainly applying algorithms to extract patterns from data.             |
| 5  | **Output**            | Useful knowledge and information.                                                            | Patterns, relationships, and models.                                  |
| 6  | **Relationship**      | The overall knowledge discovery process.                                                     | An important step within the KDD process.                             |
| 7  | **Techniques**        | Data preprocessing, data mining, interpretation, and evaluation.                             | Classification, clustering, and association.                          |
| 8  | **Human Involvement** | Requires humans to interpret and evaluate the discovered knowledge.                          | Can run as automated algorithms with less human involvement.          |
| 9  | **Focus**             | The complete process of knowledge discovery.                                                 | Mainly finding patterns in data.                                      |
| 10 | **Example**           | Discovering customer behavior and using that knowledge for business decisions.               | Finding patterns in customer purchases using association rules.       |

---

## Conclusion

> **Data Mining is a part of KDD, not a replacement for it.**
> KDD covers the whole path from raw data to usable knowledge. Data Mining is the core step in that path where patterns are found.
